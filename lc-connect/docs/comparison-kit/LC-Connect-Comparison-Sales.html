What this means

  1. Without LC Connect, the assistant answers from memory or the open web: the famous defence names, plausible guesses, and firms it says itself it cannot confirm.

Method

How we tested

The two questions (word for word)

Poland. "List companies in Poland that use pulsed laser diodes (PLD, 905 nm or 1550 nm) in their products, for example laser rangefinders, LiDAR or proximity fuzes. For each company give: name, city, the product or application, and how confident you are."

Czech Republic. The same question, with "Poland" replaced by "the Czech Republic".

When

29 September 2026, CEST (times per run in the table below). Claude's Poland run with LC Connect came after a same-day fix to how results reach Claude. ChatGPT's runs with LC Connect came after the account owner replaced ChatGPT custom instructions that had pointed it at a different connector.

With and without

  • Without LC Connect: a new chat with the LC Connect connection switched off. The assistant uses its own knowledge, and ChatGPT also searched the web on its own.
  • With LC Connect: a new chat with the connection switched on, so the assistant can look up our lead base.

Why the ChatGPT "without" answers are from the afternoon. Evening reruns with no app selected are not a fair "without": LC Connect was installed by then, and ChatGPT used it on its own (both answers open "I checked LC Connect first"). We use the afternoon runs, made before the app existed in the account.

Tip for your own test: to see a true "without" in ChatGPT, use a temporary chat or disconnect the app. An installed app may be used automatically, even when it is not selected.

Where the leads come from

How new leads get into the base: discovery, not just storage

The base is not a bought list. LC Connect has research tools that search the web for companies that fit a Laser Components product. The 396 companies in the base were found this way, country by country, then qualified and linked to a product. That is why a question like the one in this test is a quick filter, not a fresh web search, and why the answer comes back as records the team can work with.

Step 1
Research

Research tools scan the web for companies that fit a product: market analysis, application discovery, competitor analysis and lead enrichment.

Step 2
Qualify

Each candidate is linked to the LC product it fits, with sector, country, status and owner.

Step 3
Work

The team asks the assistant, assigns owners, adds notes and exports. Every change is logged.

Web research runs through an external research service and needs its key configured. The web-research step was switched off during these tests; the service was configured and verified later the same night (2026-09-29, ~23:1x CEST), after every run in this comparison.

Why the base beats reading websites

  • It is the result of that discovery work, not the LC website. 396 prospect companies in 34 countries, found and qualified for Laser Components products, each with sector, country, status and owner.
  • The assistant answers only from what is in the base and says when something is missing.
  • Structured data means filter, count and compare in one question: "Polish leads for PLDs, by status" instead of an afternoon of browsing.
  • Each user has their own account and role, and every change is logged.
  • Results come as interactive tables and maps, with CSV export.
  • This is the lead layer. Connecting the ERP and contact history is the next step.
