Backup

Security pack in one page

Sign-in: OAuth 2.1 + PKCE, HTTPS, 4 h tokens
Target: your identity provider, your MFA
Roles: ADMIN, RESEARCHER, SALES; region-scoped editing
Audit log: every write, refused attempts too
Limits: sign-in rate limits, request size limits
Logs: structured, personal data redacted
Backups: nightly, 30 days, on- and off-site
Watchdog: full sign-in check every 5 minutes
Perplexity: names, websites, questions; never notes, contacts
Hosting: your Azure or EU server; AVV annex
