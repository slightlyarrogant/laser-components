The idea

We do not train a model.
We build a harness.

The connector from the service package you received on 15 September: we call it the harness.

The engine

A model from OpenAI or Anthropic. Rented by you, under your business plan; replaceable.

The harness

Tools that give the engine your products, your lead base and your rules. Designed to learn from every correction.

Training a modelHundreds of thousands. Frozen the day it is finished.
Building a harnessImproves with daily use. No GPU purchase needed.

not on the tool.

Security pack in one page

Sign-inOAuth 2.1 with PKCE, tokens 4 h. Today: personal accounts, one per person. Target: your identity provider with your MFA.
RolesADMIN, RESEARCHER, SALES. Editing limited by ownership and region.
Audit logEvery write: who, when, before and after. Refused attempts too.
LimitsRate limits on sign-in. Size limits on every request.
LogsStructured, with personal data redacted.
TransportHTTPS end to end. Passwords stored hashed (bcrypt).
BackupsNightly, kept 30 days, on-site and off-site.
WatchdogFull sign-in check every 5 minutes. Restart and alert on failure.
Market research (Perplexity)Receives company names, websites and product/market questions; never notes, contacts or users.
HostingA: your Azure tenant. B: AutoOffice-managed server in the EU.
Data protectionData processing agreement (AVV) as an annex. Personal data stored in the EU.
ExitFull source in your repository. Data exported and deleted within 30 days.
