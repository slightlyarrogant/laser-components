Part 1Solution architecture

LC Connect is a small service that sits between the AI assistants your team uses and your own lead database. The assistant never touches the database; every request goes through the connector, which knows who is asking and what they are allowed to do.

Part 3Service level agreement · draft

Figures below are the proposed terms for discussion. Sections marked for legal review are standard clauses to be confirmed by both sides' advisers.

Parties and scope

AutoOffice (provider) operates and maintains LC Connect for Laser Components (customer). The service covers the connector software, its deployment, monitoring, maintenance, support, and the development capacity described below. It does not cover the assistant platforms, the customer's network and devices, or the identity provider, which remain the customer's own services.

Availability

  • Target: 99.5 % per calendar month for the connector, measured by the provider's automated check, which signs in and runs a query through the public address every 5 minutes.
  • Excluded from the measurement: announced maintenance, outages of the assistant platforms, the identity provider, the customer's network or hosting resources (option A), and force majeure.
  • Automated recovery runs around the clock: a failed check restarts the affected component immediately and alerts the provider.

Support and incident handling

  • Channels. The built-in reporting tool inside the assistant (report_issue), which reaches the provider's support channel directly, and email. Reports through the built-in tool carry the user, time and context automatically and are the preferred route.
  • Support hours. Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET, excluding weekends and public holidays in Poland and Germany. Response times below count in these hours.
SeverityDefinitionReactionResolution
S1 · outageService unavailable for all usersAutomated restart at once; human response within 4 support hoursRestoration target within one working day
S2 · defectA function returns wrong results or fails for some usersWithin 24 hours on working days when reported through the built-in toolFixed the same day where possible; otherwise a patch or workaround is applied and the full fix is scheduled
S3 · minorCosmetic or low-impact issueWithin 5 working daysBundled into the next maintenance release
S4 · changeNew feature or improvement requestCollected for the monthly review callDelivered from the included development hours

Maintenance and releases

  • Maintenance window: one evening per week (proposed Tuesday 20:00 to 22:00 CET), used only when needed, announced at least 48 hours ahead. Time inside the window is excluded from availability.
  • Every change is proven on staging before production. Releases are tagged in the customer's repository with release notes.
  • Changes to the assistant platforms' protocol or policies are followed within the included hours.

  • Included hours are for the month in which they accrue; up to 5 unused hours carry over to the following month. The monthly allowance can be raised by agreement.
  • Fees are invoiced monthly in advance. Invoices from AutoOffice (Poland) to Laser Components (Germany) are a reverse-charge B2B supply.
  • Fees are reviewed annually and may rise by 5 % or the consumer price index, whichever is higher.

Software licence

  • For the term of the agreement, Laser Components holds a non-exclusive licence to use LC Connect for its own business, with full source code in a repository it controls, and may let a third party operate it on its behalf.
  • Buy-out option. At any time, or on termination, Laser Components may acquire a perpetual licence to its connector for a one-off fee equal to six monthly service fees, after which no further licence fees apply. Operation and support can continue under this agreement or be taken over by another provider.
  • AutoOffice retains ownership of the underlying framework and shared components and the right to use them for other customers. Laser Components' data, configuration and knowledge base are and remain Laser Components' property.

Data protection

  • Laser Components is the controller of the lead data; AutoOffice is a processor and acts only on documented instructions. A data processing agreement (Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag) is attached as an annex and lists the sub-processors: the hosting provider, and Perplexity, which receives public company names and websites only.
  • The assistant platforms are the customer's own processors under the customer's subscriptions.
  • Personal data is stored in the EU. On termination, data is exported to the customer and deleted from the provider's systems within 30 days, backups included after their retention period.

Security

  • Sign-in via the customer's identity provider; role-based authorisation on every operation; audit trail of every write, including refused attempts.
  • Encrypted transport; secrets held outside the source code; dependencies checked for known vulnerabilities on every release.
  • Security incidents affecting customer data are reported to the customer's named contact without undue delay and within 24 hours of confirmation.

Reporting and governance

  • Monthly: uptime figure from the monitoring record, incidents and their resolution, hours used, and the change log.
  • Monthly review call (30 to 60 minutes) to prioritise change requests and plan the next month's development hours.

Term and exit

  • Initial term 12 months, then renewing for 12-month periods; termination with 3 months' notice to the end of a term.
  • On exit: the source code is already in the customer's repository; a complete database export is provided; AutoOffice supports the transition to the customer or a new provider for 30 days within the final month's included hours.

Liability legal review

Each party's liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the preceding 12 months, except for wilful misconduct, gross negligence, and breaches of data protection obligations, where statutory rules apply. Standard wording to be aligned by both parties' advisers.
