04 · Get started

Connect the demo in two minutes.

The video walks through it end to end. Here are the exact details — you enter the sign-in once, on the connector's screen, the first time you connect.

1

Turn on Developer mode

Profile (bottom-left) → Settings → Apps → Advanced settings → switch Developer mode on.

2

Create the app

Click Create app, then paste the name and connector address from the card.

3

Sign in once

Click Create — the sign-in screen opens. Enter the demo email and password. You're connected.
