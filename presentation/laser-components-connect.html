Market Context

The research layer
is being automated

The internet is increasingly automated. AI agents now research markets, competitors, and customers — not humans browsing product pages. The result: a fundamental shift in what "good data" means.

  1. The internet is being automated. AI agents research markets, map supply chains, and identify prospects programmatically. Human browsing is being replaced.
  2. Raw web data is not AI-friendly. Scattered HTML, inconsistent specs, unstructured PDFs — AI models stumble on unstructured data. They hallucinate, miss context, get specifications wrong.
  3. Structure is the foundation. Companies with clean, enriched, structured data get reliable AI results, not just better ones. That is the competitive edge.
⚡

The companies that will lead AI-driven research are not those with the most advanced models. They are those whose data is ready for AI to use.
